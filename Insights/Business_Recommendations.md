# FinNova — Business Recommendations

The recommendations below are based on the descriptive findings in the FinNova analytical model. They are areas management could consider investigating rather than guaranteed outcomes.

## 1. Strengthen Monitoring of Higher-Risk Segments

Observed default rates differ across risk segments, with higher-risk segments showing higher observed default rates.

### Suggested action
- Monitor default and delinquency by risk segment regularly.
- Combine risk grade with DTI, income, employment, loan type and customer history.
- Review whether underwriting thresholds and risk policies are producing the intended portfolio mix.

## 2. Use DPD as an Early-Warning Framework

There are **284 loans in the 61–89 DPD bucket** and **308 loans currently at 90+ DPD**.

### Suggested action
- Create an early-warning monitoring process for accounts entering 61–89 DPD.
- Track movement between DPD buckets over time.
- Monitor both account count and financial exposure.
- Separate current, early delinquency and severe delinquency reporting.

## 3. Prioritize Collections by Risk and Exposure

The portfolio contains approximately **₹22.78 Cr of overdue exposure**.

### Suggested action
- Segment collection queues using DPD severity, overdue amount and borrower/loan characteristics.
- Give additional monitoring attention to high-exposure delinquent accounts.
- Track changes in DPD and recovery after collection activity.

## 4. Evaluate Collection Effectiveness Using Outcomes

The portfolio records approximately **₹12.49 Cr** in collection amount across **31,477 actions**.

### Suggested action
Instead of comparing collection actions only by total amount collected, build outcome-based metrics such as:

- Recovery rate
- Amount recovered / collectible amount
- Promise-to-Pay kept rate
- Cure rate
- Roll-forward / roll-back rate
- Recovery by DPD bucket
- Recovery by collection action

This would provide a more complete view of collection performance.

## 5. Monitor Customer-Type Risk

The project shows differences between new and existing customers in approval and observed default rates.

### Suggested action
- Monitor portfolio quality separately for new and existing customers.
- Compare default, DPD and recovery metrics by customer type.
- Investigate whether customer tenure, prior repayment behavior or exposure explains observed differences.

## 6. Monitor Acquisition Channels

Observed default rates vary across acquisition channels.

### Suggested action
- Monitor channel-level application volume, approval rate, disbursement and default rate together.
- Compare channels using comparable customer and loan segments.
- Avoid evaluating a channel using default rate alone because portfolio composition can differ.

## 7. Combine Growth and Risk KPIs

Portfolio growth should be viewed together with credit-risk indicators.

### Suggested management scorecard

Track:

- Total loans
- Disbursed amount
- Average loan amount
- Default rate
- Delinquency rate
- 90+ DPD loans
- 90+ DPD exposure
- Overdue amount
- Outstanding principal
- Collection amount
- Recovery outcomes

## 8. Improve Cohort Monitoring

Recent loans have had less time to reach severe delinquency than older loans.

### Suggested action
- Compare cohorts using appropriate seasoning periods.
- Avoid directly comparing a newly originated cohort with a fully matured cohort.
- Track cumulative delinquency/default development by origination month.

## 9. Improve Risk Reporting Beyond a Single Score

Risk grade is useful for segmentation but should not be treated as the only risk indicator.

### Suggested action
Use a combined view of:

- Credit score
- Debt-to-income ratio
- Monthly income
- Employment history
- Customer type
- Loan amount
- Loan type
- Interest rate
- Tenure
- Previous repayment behavior
- DPD history

## 10. Recommended Management Review

A recurring management review could follow this structure:

### Portfolio
How much was originated and disbursed?

### Risk
What is the current default and delinquency position?

### Exposure
Where is the overdue financial exposure concentrated?

### Collections
What amount was collected and what recovery outcomes occurred?

### Segments
Which risk, customer and acquisition segments require further investigation?

### Trend
Are portfolio and risk indicators improving or deteriorating over time?

## Important Note

These recommendations are based on a synthetic portfolio and descriptive analysis. They should be validated against real portfolio behavior, business constraints, regulatory requirements, credit policy and operational capacity before implementation.
