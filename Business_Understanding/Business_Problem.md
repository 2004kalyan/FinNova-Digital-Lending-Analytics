# Business Problem

## Project
**FinNova Digital Lending, Credit Risk & Collections Analytics**

## Business Context
FinNova is a fictional digital lending business that provides loans through digital channels. This project uses synthetically generated data because real customer-level lending data is confidential.

## Problem Statement
The business needs to understand performance across the complete lending lifecycle:

**Application → Credit Assessment → Approval → Disbursement → Repayment → Delinquency → Collections → Default**

The key challenge is balancing loan growth with credit quality and effective collections.

Management needs visibility into:
- Applications and approval performance
- Disbursement volume and value
- Credit-risk segmentation
- Delinquency and DPD movement
- Default exposure
- Outstanding and overdue amounts
- Collection activity and collected amounts
- Differences across customer, loan, risk, and acquisition segments

## Business Objective
Build an analytics solution that converts lending and repayment data into actionable insights for credit-risk and collections decision-making.

The analysis focuses on:
1. Measuring overall portfolio performance.
2. Identifying risk segments associated with higher observed default rates.
3. Monitoring delinquency and DPD buckets.
4. Quantifying overdue and outstanding exposure.
5. Understanding collections activity and collected amounts.
6. Creating an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Key Business Risk
Rapid loan growth without adequate risk monitoring can increase delinquency, default exposure, and collection requirements.

Therefore, management should monitor both **portfolio growth and portfolio quality**.

## Analytical Note
The dataset is synthetic and observational. Segment-level differences should be interpreted as associations, not proof of causation.
