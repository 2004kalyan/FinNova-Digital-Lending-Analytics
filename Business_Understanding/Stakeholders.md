# Stakeholders

## 1. Head of Credit / Credit Risk Manager
**Focus:** Credit quality and lending risk.

**Questions:**
- Which risk segments have higher observed default rates?
- How many loans are delinquent?
- What is the 90+ DPD exposure?
- How is portfolio risk distributed?

**KPIs:** Default Rate, Delinquency Rate, Average DPD, 90+ DPD Loans, 90+ DPD Exposure, Credit Score, DTI.

## 2. Collections Manager
**Focus:** Delinquency management and recovery.

**Questions:**
- How many loans require collection activity?
- Which DPD buckets have collection exposure?
- How much has been collected?
- Which collection actions are being used?

**KPIs:** Total Collection Amount, Collection Actions, Loans with Collection Activity, Overdue Amount, DPD Bucket, Recovery Status.

## 3. Business / Lending Head
**Focus:** Portfolio growth and quality.

**Questions:**
- How many loans are disbursed?
- What is the total disbursed amount?
- What is the average loan size?
- Is growth accompanied by acceptable credit quality?

**KPIs:** Total Loans, Total Disbursed Amount, Average Loan Amount, Approval Rate, Disbursement Rate, Default Rate.

## 4. Finance Team
**Focus:** Financial exposure.

**Questions:**
- How much principal is outstanding?
- How much is overdue?
- How much has been paid?
- What is current financial exposure?

**KPIs:** Outstanding Principal, Overdue Amount, Amount Due, Amount Paid, Collection Amount.

## 5. Marketing / Acquisition Team
**Focus:** Acquisition-channel performance.

**Questions:**
- Which channels generate the most loans?
- How do default rates differ across channels?
- Which channels need portfolio-quality monitoring?

**KPIs:** Applications, Approval Rate, Disbursed Loans, Disbursed Amount, Default Rate by Channel.

## 6. Data / BI Analyst
**Focus:** Reliable reporting and business insights.

**Responsibilities:**
- Data cleaning and quality validation
- Exploratory data analysis
- SQL transformation
- KPI development
- Data modeling
- Power BI dashboard development
- Insight generation and reporting

## Stakeholder Summary

| Stakeholder | Primary Focus |
|---|---|
| Credit / Risk | Credit quality and default risk |
| Collections | Delinquency and recovery |
| Business / Lending | Portfolio growth |
| Finance | Financial exposure |
| Marketing / Acquisition | Channel performance |
| Data / BI | Analytics and reporting |
