# Business Questions

## 1. Portfolio Performance
- How many loans have been disbursed?
- What is the total disbursed amount?
- What is the average loan amount?
- What is the approval rate?
- What is the application-to-disbursement conversion?

## 2. Credit Risk
- How does default rate vary across credit-risk grades?
- Which risk segments have higher observed default rates?
- How does credit score relate to portfolio outcomes?
- Does debt-to-income ratio show meaningful differences?
- How does default rate vary by customer type?

## 3. Delinquency & DPD
- How many loans are currently delinquent?
- What percentage of loans are delinquent?
- How many loans are currently at 90+ DPD?
- What is the 90+ DPD exposure?
- How are loans distributed across DPD buckets?
- What is the maximum observed DPD?

## 4. Collections
- How much has been collected?
- How many loans have collection activity?
- How does collection amount vary by DPD bucket?
- How many collection actions have been performed?
- How is activity distributed across collection actions?
- What is the distribution of recovered versus partial outcomes?

## 5. Acquisition & Customer Segments
- How does default rate vary by acquisition channel?
- How does default rate vary between new and existing customers?
- How does portfolio performance vary by loan type?
- Which customer segments show meaningful differences in observed outcomes?

## 6. Management Monitoring
- Is portfolio growth accompanied by changes in credit quality?
- Where is overdue exposure concentrated?
- Which segments require closer monitoring?
- What areas should credit and collections teams investigate further?

## Analytical Principles
These questions support descriptive and diagnostic analytics.

Segment-level differences are treated as **associations rather than causal effects** unless additional evidence is available.

For this project, **90+ DPD is the project-specific default convention**. It should not be interpreted as a universal regulatory definition.
